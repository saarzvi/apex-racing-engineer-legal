Accessibility

Updated September 24, 2026

OverviewPrivacyTermsSupport

We aim to make the APEX Racing Engineer website and support information usable with keyboards, zoom, high-contrast displays and common assistive technologies. We are continuing to test and improve accessibility and do not claim certification or full conformance with a particular standard unless independently verified.

Current measures

  • Semantic headings, links and navigation.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Improved text contrast for secondary information.
  • Responsive layout intended to reflow on smaller screens and at increased zoom.
  • A skip link to bypass repeated navigation.

Need help?

If a page, control or document is difficult to use, email Cornel_reddy_3j@icloud.com. Please describe the page or task and the accessibility barrier you encountered. We will use the report to investigate and improve the experience.
