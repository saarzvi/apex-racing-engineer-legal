APEX Racing Engineer is sold as a one-time paid download. Version 1.0 has no subscription and no in-app purchases. Payments are processed by the app store through which you purchase the App; this website does not process payment-card information.

Apple App Store

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are billed and administered by Apple. Refund eligibility and processing are handled through Apple’s official purchase and refund systems. If you believe a purchase should be refunded, use Apple’s official refund process or contact Apple Support. The Developer cannot directly reverse an App Store transaction.

Google Play

Purchases made through Google Play are billed and administered through Google Play. Refund eligibility may depend on the timing and circumstances of the request, Google Play policies and applicable law. Start with Google Play’s official refund process. If Google directs you to the Developer or the store process does not resolve the issue, contact us through the private developer contact shown on the store listing.

Technical problems

If the App materially fails to operate as described on a supported device and configuration, contact support and tell us the platform, App version and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, full payment-card information, console IP addresses or telemetry recordings unless specifically requested through a private support process.

Consumer rights

Nothing on this page limits any mandatory refund, cancellation, withdrawal, warranty or other consumer right that applies where you live. Store policies and applicable law prevail where they provide additional rights.

Contact

For unresolved purchase questions, use the private developer contact shown on the relevant App Store or Google Play listing. Public GitHub issues should not be used for order references, payment information or other private purchase information.
