Summary

APEX Racing Engineer is a local-first, independent multi-title racing telemetry and race-engineer platform. Version 1.0.0 does not require an account or contain advertising or analytics. Driving telemetry and recordings stay on your device. If you choose to submit in-app feedback, the report is sent to the Developer for support.

Data handled on your device

  • The local network address or connection information you enter for a supported telemetry source.
  • Racing telemetry received from a supported game or device through its supported connection method.
  • Session recordings and metadata, including timestamps, lap information, packet format and console IP.
  • Preferences such as voice callouts and cockpit theme.
  • Manual pit, fuel and tyre selections while a session is active.

This information stays on your device unless you independently export, copy or share it. Recordings are limited to the 12 most recent completed sessions; older recordings are automatically removed.

Collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share your locally stored connection information, racing telemetry, recordings or preferences. The App has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK in version 1.0.0. Direct communication with a supported local telemetry source is not collection by us.

Optional feedback

If you choose to use the in-app feedback form, we receive the category you select, the text you enter, the App version and your platform (for example iOS or Android). We do not attach your email address, console IP, telemetry, recordings or a device identifier to that report. Feedback is used only to investigate problems, provide support and consider feature requests. The delivery relay uses Google Apps Script and Gmail as service providers to deliver the report to the Developer’s support mailbox. GitHub Pages hosts the public legal/support pages. These providers may process ordinary technical information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Voice callouts

Voice callouts use the text-to-speech service provided by your device. Its operation, including optional network processing selected in device settings, is governed by the operating-system or speech-provider terms. We do not receive callout text or speech output.

External links and hosting

Opening Privacy Policy, Terms or Support links launches your browser. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive ordinary web-request information such as IP address, browser details and access time under GitHub’s privacy statement. We add no cookies, tracking scripts or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until deleted in the App, automatically pruned, cleared by the operating system, or removed when the App is uninstalled. Feedback and ordinary support messages you voluntarily submit are retained for up to 90 days, then deleted from the active support mailbox and relay records, unless a longer period is reasonably required for an unresolved legal, security, fraud, refund, accounting or regulatory matter. Use System → Privacy & Legal → Delete Local Session Data to delete recordings, the saved console IP and App preferences.

Because we do not receive your local telemetry, recordings or preferences, we generally cannot access, correct, export or delete that local data for you. Feedback submitted through the support form is separate. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of a support message where applicable by emailing Cornel_reddy_3j@icloud.com. Because feedback is intentionally not linked to an account or device identifier, we may need information from you that reasonably identifies the specific message before we can act on a request.

Security

We minimize permissions, disable Android backup for App data, exclude iOS session recordings from iCloud backup, keep components non-exported where appropriate, and do not enable cleartext web traffic. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure; protect access to your device and local network.

Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when App behavior or legal requirements change. The effective date identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, email Cornel_reddy_3j@icloud.com. Do not include passwords, console IP addresses, telemetry recordings or other sensitive information unless specifically requested through a private support process.
