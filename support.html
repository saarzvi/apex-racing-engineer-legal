Before requesting help

  • Place the phone or tablet and PlayStation on the same normal LAN; guest Wi-Fi and client isolation block telemetry.
  • Confirm the console IPv4 address in PlayStation network settings.
  • Start a driveable Gran Turismo 7 session, enter the address, and select Start Live.
  • Stop other telemetry tools that may already use UDP port 33740.
  • On iOS, allow Local Network access and keep the App in the foreground during live telemetry.

Contact support

Open a support request and include the App version, device model, operating-system version, connection status and steps to reproduce.

Do not post console IP addresses, telemetry recordings, account details or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Product boundary

APEX Racing Engineer is an independent, unofficial local-network companion. It has no account, subscription, advertising or developer-operated telemetry service.
