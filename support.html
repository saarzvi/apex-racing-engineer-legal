Supported games

APEX Racing Engineer is built as a multi-title telemetry platform. Version 1.0 launches with Gran Turismo 7 support on PlayStation; additional supported games and telemetry sources may be added in later releases.

Before requesting help — Gran Turismo 7

  • Place the phone or tablet and PlayStation on the same normal LAN; guest Wi-Fi and client isolation block telemetry.
  • Confirm the console IPv4 address in PlayStation network settings.
  • Start a driveable Gran Turismo 7 session, enter the address, and select Start Live.
  • Stop other telemetry tools that may already use UDP port 33740.
  • On iOS, allow Local Network access and keep the App in the foreground during live telemetry.

Contact support

The preferred support path is the in-app form under System → Feedback & Reviews. Choose Bug, Problem or Feature Request and describe the issue in text. The App sends only that report, its category, App version and platform.

If the in-app form is unavailable, open a support request.

Do not include console IP addresses, telemetry recordings, passwords, account details or other sensitive information.

Product boundary

APEX Racing Engineer is an independent multi-title racing telemetry platform. Supported-game and platform names identify compatibility only. The App has no account, subscription, advertising or developer-operated telemetry service.

Purchases and refunds

APEX Racing Engineer is sold as a one-time paid download through supported app stores. See Purchases & Refunds for cancellation and refund information.
